Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open any standard web browser on your computer, tablet, or phone.
  2. Read through the initial disclaimer screen when the page loads. Note that the tool runs entirely inside your browser session, meaning no personal information, text, or uploads are ever saved on a central server.
  3. Choose how to check the suspicious message:
    • Option A: Copy the text from the suspicious email, text message, or letter and paste it right into the box below.
    • Option B: Take a picture or screenshot of the message and upload the image file using the upload button.
  4. Click the analyze button to check the message for multi-word threat patterns or scam indicators.
  5. Review the result:
    • If no multi-word scam patterns show up, the tool will let you know.
    • If a scam is found, a guidance box will appear right away to walk you through what to do and connect you with external resource channels.
  6. Click the print reference guide button if you want a paper copy of the emergency numbers and trusted web links to keep by your phone.
  7. Open your browser menu on a mobile device and select "Add to Home Screen" if you want to pin the tool to your phone like a regular app.

📲 How to Report Scam Text Messages (7726)

If you receive a suspicious text message, you can report it instantly to mobile carriers and security authorities without clicking any links:

1. Copy or Screenshot: Do not tap any links inside the message.

2. Forward to 7726: Open your text message app, press and hold the suspicious text message, select "Forward", and send it to the short code 7726 (spells "SPAM").

3. Follow-up: Your carrier will reply asking for the sender's phone number. Reply with the number that sent the text to complete the block.
